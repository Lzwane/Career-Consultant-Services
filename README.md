# CAREER CONSULTATION SERVICES

## Website Development Brief

### 1. About the Business

**Career Consultation Services** is an education and application support business that assists learners with identifying suitable tertiary education opportunities and completing applications to institutions.

The business is focused on simplifying the application process for learners by allowing them to submit their academic results and required supporting documents through the website.

Once a learner submits their information and documents, Career Consultation Services will assess the learner's results and determine which institutions and study opportunities the learner may qualify for. Staff members will then use the documents submitted by the learner to assist with the relevant applications.

The website is therefore designed to connect learners with the application support services provided by Career Consultation Services.

### 2. Name of the Website

**Career Consultation Services**

The website should use **Career Consultation Services** as the official business name throughout the website.

### 3. Main Purpose of the Website

The main purpose of the website is to provide learners with a simple and convenient way to submit their academic results and supporting documents so that Career Consultation Services can determine their eligibility for tertiary institutions and assist them with applications.

The website should allow learners to:

* Create or submit an application enquiry.

* Upload their academic results.

* Upload their matric certificate or other relevant academic documents.

* Upload a copy of their ID.

* Upload proof of payment.

* Upload other documents required for applications.

* Submit all their documents securely.

* Receive information about which institutions they may qualify for based on their results.

After submission, **Career Consultation Services staff will access the uploaded documents through the website's back end**.

Staff will then:

1. Review the learner's uploaded documents.

2. Check the learner's academic results.

3. Determine which institutions and programmes the learner qualifies for based on the available requirements.

4. Use the learner's submitted documents to assist with the relevant applications.

5. Manage and track the learner's application process internally.

### 4. Website Workflow

The website should have two main sides:

#### A. Learner Front End

This is the section that learners will interact with.

The learner should be able to:

**Step 1 – Submit Details**

The learner provides their basic information, such as:

* Full name.

* ID number.

* Contact number.

* Email address.

* Grade/status.

* Other relevant personal information.

**Step 2 – Upload Documents**

The learner uploads the required documents, which may include:

* Copy of ID.

* Matric certificate.

* Academic results.

* Latest school results, where applicable.

* Proof of payment.

* Other supporting documents required for the application.

**Step 3 – Submit**

The learner submits all their information and documents through the website.

**Step 4 – Eligibility Assessment**

Career Consultation Services reviews the learner's results and determines which institutions and relevant study opportunities the learner may qualify for.

The website should present the eligibility information in a clear and understandable way.

### B. Staff Back End

The back end will be used by Career Consultation Services staff.

Staff should be able to:

* Log into a secure staff/admin area.

* View learner profiles.

* View submitted applications.

* Access uploaded documents.

* Download documents where necessary.

* Review academic results.

* Record institutions the learner qualifies for.

* Record application information.

* Update the learner's application status.

* Keep track of applications being processed.

* Communicate relevant updates to the learner.

The back end should provide staff with an organised dashboard so that learner applications and documents can be managed efficiently.

### 5. Key Website Pages

#### Home Page

The home page should introduce Career Consultation Services and clearly explain what the business does.

It should include:

* Career Consultation Services logo.

* Short introduction to the business.

* Explanation of the application-support service.

* How the process works.

* "Get Started" or "Submit Your Documents" button.

* Contact information.

Suggested message:

**"Submit Your Results. Know Where You Qualify. Let Us Assist With Your Application."**

#### About Us

This page should explain:

* Who Career Consultation Services is.

* What the business does.

* The purpose of the service.

* How the business assists learners.

* The company's mission and vision.

#### How It Works

This page should clearly explain the process:

**1. Submit your information**

↓

**2. Upload your documents**

↓

**3. We assess your results**

↓

**4. We identify institutions you may qualify for**

↓

**5. Our consultants assist with your applications**

#### Submit Documents

This should be one of the main sections of the website.

Learners should be able to upload their required documents through a secure form.

The page should include:

* Personal information form.

* Document upload fields.

* Proof of payment upload.

* Submit button.

* Confirmation after successful submission.

#### Eligibility / Qualification Results

After the learner's information has been assessed, the website should allow the relevant eligibility information to be recorded and displayed.

The information may include:

* Institution.

* Programme/course.

* Qualification requirements.

* Whether the learner meets the available academic requirements.

* Application status.

The website should make it clear that eligibility information is based on the requirements available at the time of assessment and that final admission decisions remain with the relevant institution.

#### Application Status

Learners should be able to check the progress of their applications.

Possible statuses could include:

* Documents Submitted.

* Under Assessment.

* Institution Identified.

* Application in Progress.

* Application Submitted.

* Awaiting Response.

* Successful.

* Unsuccessful.

* Additional Documents Required.

#### Contact Us

The contact page should include:

* Phone number.

* Email address.

* WhatsApp option, if applicable.

* Social media links.

* Contact form.

### 6. Important Features

The website should include:

* Learner document upload system.

* Secure document storage.

* Learner information forms.

* Staff/admin login.

* Staff dashboard.

* Document management.

* Eligibility assessment records.

* Institution and programme information.

* Application tracking.

* Application status updates.

* Proof of payment verification.

* Notifications or communication between staff and learners, if feasible.

* Mobile-friendly design.

### 7. Features That Have Been Removed

The following features are **not required** in the new website:

* ❌ Artificial Intelligence (AI).

* ❌ AI career guidance.

* ❌ Career guidance services through the website.

* ❌ Automatic/auto-apply functionality.

* ❌ Automatic submission of applications by the system.

* ❌ Mobile application development.

Applications will be handled by **Career Consultation Services staff** using the documents and information submitted by the learner.

### 8. Logo and Images

The website should use the official **Career Consultation Services** logo once provided.

The visual identity should communicate:

* Education.

* Opportunity.

* Professionalism.

* Trust.

* Accessibility.

* Academic progression.

Suitable images may include learners, students, tertiary institutions, education, applications and academic achievement.

**Logo:** To be provided separately.

**Additional images:** To be selected or provided during the website design process.

### 9. Website Design

The website should have a **professional, modern and simple design** that is easy for learners to use.

Because many users will access the website from mobile phones, the website must be fully responsive and mobile-friendly.

The interface should make the document-upload process particularly simple and clear.

The main call-to-action should be:

**"Submit Your Documents"**

A secondary call-to-action can be:

**"Check Where You Qualify"**

### 10. Overall Website Objective

The overall objective of the website is to create a central online system where learners can submit their academic results and supporting documents, while Career Consultation Services staff can review those documents, determine suitable institutional opportunities based on the learner's results, and assist with the relevant applications.

The website is therefore primarily an **education application-support and document-management system**, rather than a career-guidance or AI-based platform.
